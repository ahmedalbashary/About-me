Search Optimization AI Query Mapping Kuwait

How AI-Powered Search Optimization Lifted CVR by 49% in 3 Months

Gaming & PC accessories retailer — Kuwait · 3 offline branches · Large online catalog

🔍 The Problem

Behavioral research revealed that the majority of users relied heavily on the search bar to navigate the catalog — it was their primary tool. But the search was failing them: returning irrelevant results, empty pages, and mismatched products due to poor query mapping and missing synonyms.

Users were dropping off not because the products weren't there — but because the search couldn't connect their intent to the inventory.

⚙️ What We Did

  • Analyzed zero-results queries and mismatched search terms across the catalog
  • Built a comprehensive synonym library covering gaming terminology, brand names, and colloquial terms
  • Restructured search rules to prioritize relevance over exact match
  • Integrated AI-powered query mapping to interpret user intent and surface the right products

📈 The Result

+49% Search CVR (1.6% → 2.38%)
$25K+ Incremental monthly revenue
3 months Time to result

UVP Testing A/B Test Saudi Arabia

One Question Changed Everything: Why Do Customers Actually Buy From Us?

Well-known coffee brand — Saudi Arabia · Multiple physical branches · Established ecommerce store

🔍 The Problem

At the start of this engagement, we faced a question most brands never actually answer: why do customers buy from us?

Is it the price? The promotions? The free shipping? Instead of guessing, we decided to find out. We analyzed on-site behavior and ran a customer poll directly on the site. The answer was clear — customers weren't looking for the cheapest option. They wanted the best product and real value after purchase.

The announcement bar was promoting free shipping. But free shipping wasn't the reason people were buying. We were leading with the wrong message.

⚙️ What We Did

  • Ran an on-site customer poll to understand the primary purchase drivers
  • Analyzed session recordings and heatmaps to identify where trust hesitation was occurring
  • Designed an A/B test: Control (free shipping announcement bar) vs. Variant (sticky UVP bar highlighting the brand's true differentiators)
  • Placed the UVP bar sitewide — persistent across product pages, cart, and checkout — to reinforce trust at every decision point

📈 The Result

+43% CVR uplift (variant vs. control)
Sitewide Impact across full funnel

Key insight: Customers don't always tell you what drives them to buy — but their behavior does. The poll confirmed what the data was hinting at: the message mattered more than the promotion.

Homepage Redesign for Egypt's Largest Pharmacy Chain: +25% CVR in Month One

Large pharmacy chain — Egypt · 20+ branches across multiple governorates · High-traffic website

🔍 The Problem

Analytics and behavioral research showed that users weren't finding what they needed quickly after landing on the homepage. The hero section wasn't communicating relevance — it wasn't guiding user intent, and the search bar wasn't prominent enough for users who came with a specific product in mind.

The result: high bounce rates and users abandoning before reaching any product.

⚙️ What We Did

  • Conducted full behavioral analysis: GA4 funnel data, heatmaps, and session recordings
  • Identified what users actually needed above the fold based on their search and navigation patterns
  • Redesigned the hero banner to communicate the pharmacy's value and guide user intent immediately
  • Repositioned and visually elevated the search bar based on how users actually navigated

📈 The Result

+25% CVR increase
Month 1 Time to result

Key insight: The homepage is a promise. It tells users: "You're in the right place, and here's what to do next." When that promise is unclear, users leave — not because the products aren't there, but because the page didn't earn their next click.

Building a $3M Ecommerce Brand From Scratch Through Continuous Experimentation

Design Kaf — Egypt · Fashion ecommerce · 200K+ followers · 5 physical branches

🔍 The Context

Design Kaf wasn't a client — it was my own brand, built from zero. Which meant every decision about CRO, product strategy, and site optimization was mine to make and mine to live with. There was no safety net. Every test either moved the needle or cost us revenue.

As the brand grew, conversion and order value plateaued. The site needed to evolve continuously — not just once.

⚙️ What We Did

  • Built a continuous experimentation rhythm: regular research on trending products, colors, and customer preferences
  • Applied psychological triggers (scarcity, social proof, authority) to product pages and offer structures
  • Optimized AOV through pricing psychology and upsell flows
  • Ran analytics-driven audits combining quantitative data and qualitative research
  • Continuously updated and refined Shopify product pages based on test results

📈 The Result

+50% Conversion rate growth
+30% Average order value
$3M+ Annual revenue
200K+ Social followers

Key insight: CRO isn't a project — it's a practice. The brands that win aren't the ones who ran one great test. They're the ones who built a system that continuously learns, adapts, and compounds results over time.
