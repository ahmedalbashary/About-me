About Me

I'm Ahmed.
I help ecommerce brands convert.

I'm a CRO Specialist based in Cairo, Egypt. For the past 2+ years I've been helping ecommerce brands in Egypt and Saudi Arabia turn traffic into revenue — through structured experimentation, behavioral analytics, and a genuine obsession with understanding why people buy.

Before that, I spent 7 years building my own DTC brand from scratch — Design Kaf — scaling it to $3M+ in annual sales and 5 physical branches. That experience changed how I think about CRO. I don't just analyze sites. I've lived the pressure of a business owner watching every metric.

How I Work

My process starts with data, not opinions. Every recommendation I make is backed by behavioral analysis — GA4, Hotjar, Clarity — combined with heuristic audits using Nielsen's 10 Usability Principles and Speero methodology.

Then I apply behavioral science. Cialdini's Principles, the Fogg Behavior Model, cognitive biases — these aren't just frameworks I've read about. They're the lens I use to form every hypothesis before we run a test.

The output is a structured CRO roadmap: insight → hypothesis → ICE prioritization → A/B execution → results communicated in plain language that connects user behavior to revenue impact.
